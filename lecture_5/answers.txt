The public IP did not change after enabling the VPN. This is most likely
because the VPN is configured with split tunneling, meaning only traffic
destined for specific (university) resources is routed through the VPN,
while general internet requests such as curl ifconfig.me still go out via
my normal ISP connection. As a result, the internet-facing IP address
remains the ISP-assigned one. The local IP and default gateway also stay
the same because they describe my local network, which the VPN does not
alter.

4.5 Trace Route to Google
The First hub is my default gateway: 192.168.1.1

The Last hub is a Google server: 172.217.171.99

Other observations:
- Hop count: ~10-15 hops.
- Latency increases with each hop because packets travel further
  and through more routers.
- Some hops show "* * *" meaning they did not reply to the ICMP
  request (this is normal and not an error).
